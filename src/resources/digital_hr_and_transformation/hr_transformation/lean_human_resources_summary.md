# Executive Thesis
In *Lean Human Resources*, Cheryl Jekiel argues that HR is the engine of a Lean enterprise. The core thesis is that Lean is not just for manufacturing; it is a cultural and operational system that requires HR to redesign its own processes (recruitment, talent development, performance) to eliminate waste and deliver maximum value to the business. HR must lead the "people side" of Lean transformation.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The Lean HR Transformation Map**: A multi-stage roadmap for embedding Lean principles into people operations.
*   **Calculating Potential Improvement Value (PIV)**: A methodology for quantifying the hidden costs of inefficient HR processes.
*   **The Lean Leadership Development Model**: Frameworks for building leaders who coach and enable rather than command and control.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Eliminate the "Seven Wastes" in HR: Over-processing, Waiting, Motion, Inventory, Defects, Over-production, and Transportation.
*   Focus on "Standard Work" for HR transactions to provide a stable foundation for strategic innovation.
