## 1.2 Research questions 

While there were separate bodies of research in a variety of subject areas related to this research, there was limited literature bringing together the issue of the impact of the architecture of the HRM function on key stakeholder groups of HR professionals, senior managers and employees in a public sector setting in Australia. The primary objective of this study is to consider and question HRM architecture in the Australian public sector and the positive and negative implications for key stakeholders. 

Research questions to be explored are: 

a. What are the driving forces of the adoption of different models of HRM architecture in the public sector in Australia? 

b. What is the impact of the HRM architecture on the career of HR professionals? 

c. What is the experience of senior managers of different HRM architecture? 

d. What are employees’ experiences of such HRM architecture?
