# Executive Thesis
Clarke's work on *Flexible Human Resource Service Delivery Models* argues that traditional, rigid HR "Silos" are no longer fit for purpose in complex, large-scale organisations (specifically the Australian Public Sector). The thesis is that HR success hinges on "Agile Service Delivery"—a model that balances centralised efficiency (GBS/Shared Services) with decentralised flexibility (embedded HR Business Partners). This is a foundational text for modern HR Operating Models.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The Hybrid Service Model**: Centralised Centres of Excellence (CoE) and Shared Services combined with Agile BP pools.
*   **Stakeholder Expectation Matrix**: Mapping the unique needs of senior leaders, middle managers, and frontline employees against HR capabilities.
*   **The Scalability Loop**: A cyclical approach to expanding and contracting HR services based on organisational life cycles.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Establish clear Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for transactional tasks to free up HRBP capacity for strategic work.
*   Develop a "Single Source of Truth" for HR data to provide consistency across all distributed service delivery points.
