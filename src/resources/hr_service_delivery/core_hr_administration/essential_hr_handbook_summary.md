# Executive Thesis
*The Essential HR Handbook* argues that the core of HR excellence lies in its foundational transactions. The authors suggest that strategic HR is only possible when transactional "table stakes"—recruitment, performance, and legal compliance—are executed with flawless efficiency. This handbook provides the pragmatic tools and workflows to manage the complete employee lifecycle from a high-quality operational perspective.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The Employee Lifecycle Model**: Attraction, Recruitment, Onboarding, Development, Retention, Offboarding.
*   **Performance Management Loop**: Goal setting, Real-time feedback, and Formal evaluation.
*   **Talent Acquisition Strategy**: Sourcing and selection methods for a candidate-centric market.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Master the functional basics (payroll, benefits, compliance) to earn the right to influence business strategy.
*   Prioritise the onboarding experience as the critical predictor of long-term employee retention.
