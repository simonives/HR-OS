# Executive Thesis
The *Labour and Employment Law Manual* by Joydeep Hor argues that employment law is a strategic lever, not just a compliance obligation. The thesis is that by deeply understanding the legal frameworks—from the inception of the employment relationship to its termination—HR leaders can proactively manage risk and build more stable industrial relations environments.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The Contract of Employment Model**: Inherent and expressed terms and conditions.
*   **Industrial Relations Strategy**: Managing collective bargaining and trade union interactions.
*   **The Dispute Resolution Hierarchy**: Methods for internal and external grievance handling.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Master the drafting of employment contracts to provide ultimate operational flexibility.
*   Proactively manage industrial risk through continuous consultation and genuine engagement.
