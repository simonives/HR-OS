This doesn’t need to be a complicated and convoluted process. It’s a

bit like going out on a date. When most people first start dating, they’re

oftentimes stuck worrying about what to say. The common dating advice

passed around is simply to ask the other person about himself or herself

as a way to start the conversation. Use this same approach to learn about

your employees. Start by asking a simple question, something along the

lines of “During your time at our organization, what particular moments

or events have shaped your experience the most?”
