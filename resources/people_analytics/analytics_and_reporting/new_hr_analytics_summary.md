# Executive Thesis
In *The New HR Analytics*, Jac Fitz-Enz (widely considered the "father of HR metrics") argues that HR must move from activity-based reporting to value-based prediction. The core thesis is that human capital is the only asset that can be truly predicted to increase in value if managed through rigorous economic modelling and predictive analytics. The goal is to prove the ROI of human capital investments.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The HCM:21® Model**: A 21st-century framework for Human Capital Management that shifts from metrics to analytics.
*   **The Predictive Analytics Process (PAP)**: A structured cycle for identifying business questions, selecting data, and predicting future performance.
*   **Human Capital Value Added (HCVA)**: A formulaic approach to quantifying the net economic contribution of the workforce.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Stop measuring what HR *does* and start measuring what HR *delivers* in economic terms.
*   Use predictive modelling to anticipate talent shortages and productivity gaps before they impact the P&L.
