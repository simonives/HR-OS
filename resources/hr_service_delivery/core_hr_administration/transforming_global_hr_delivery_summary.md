# Executive Thesis
This research document argues that modern global organisations must move away from locally managed HR processes towards a unified, Global Business Services (GBS) model. The core thesis is that Lean Six Sigma methodology—Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control (DMAIC)—is the optimal framework for achieving global standardisation, reducing error rates in payroll and administration, and delivering a consistent employee experience across borders.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The Global Standardisation Framework**: A methodology for identifying and harmonising across varied local HR processes.
*   **The DMAIC in HR Implementation**: Applying Six Sigma rigor to human resource transactions (hiring, offboarding, talent data).
*   **Service Level Agreements (SLA) & Key Performance Indicators (KPI)**: Metrics for measuring the success of a global shared service delivery model.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Establish a "Single Global Process Owner" for each HR transaction to ensure cross-border consistency.
*   Use data to identify bottlenecks in the employee lifecycle and apply root-cause analysis to eliminate them.
