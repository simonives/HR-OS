# Executive Thesis
*Expanding Human Resource Risk Management Systems* argues that HR is a critical component of institutional risk (Operational Risk). The core thesis is that HR must build sophisticated risk models to identify, quantify, and mitigate "People Risk"—specifically in the context of high-compliance industries like banking. This involves moving beyond basic compliance and into predictive models for turnover, misconduct, and regulatory alignment.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The HR Risk Taxonomy**: Defining categories of risk across performance, compliance, legal, and operational domains.
*   **Mitigation Hierarchies**: Prioritising risks based on likelihood, impact, and regulatory exposure.
*   **HR Governance Structure**: Designing a "Three Lines of Defence" model for personnel-related risk.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Incorporate people risk into the broader enterprise risk management (ERM) framework.
*   Proactively monitor and audit "Key Risk Indicators" (KRIs) within the workforce to prevent systemic failure.
