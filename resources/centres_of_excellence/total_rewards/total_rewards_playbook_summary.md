# Executive Thesis
The *Total Rewards Playbook* by Oyster posits that for the modern, global, and often remote workforce, compensation must move beyond simple base salaries. The core thesis is that a "Total Rewards" strategy—encompassing equity, flexible benefits, local compliance, and work-life harmony—is the primary competitive advantage in the global war for talent. Organisations must build scalable, transparent systems that treat "Rewards" as a holistic employee value proposition.

## Key Architectures & Frameworks
*   **The Total Rewards Equation**: Salary + Equity + Benefits + Environment + Growth.
*   **Global Benchmarking Lifecycle**: Methods for locally competitive reward design across borders.
*   **Equity as an Incentive**: Frameworks for stock options, RSUs, and ownership culture.

## Strategic Imperatives
*   Design for parity, not just equality, in global compensation to account for local cost-of-living and tax realities.
*   Clearly articulate the full value of the reward package to employees (EVP) to maximise its motivational impact.
